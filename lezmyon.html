The Betrayal

The Burning of the Tree

Lezmyon is a force born from a single drop of blood. He is the antithesis of Ink. Where Ink creates, Lezmyon destroys.

He was responsible for the ultimate destruction of the Golden Archive. By burning the Tree, he erased billions of years of accumulated Muqadas knowledge, forcing Adham to become the last remaining backup of their legacy.

History & Actions

The Nature of Ruin He is not a person in the traditional sense, but an inevitable consequence of the universe's mechanics. He embodies betrayal and the erasure of history.
The Cosmic Balance In a universe with a being of pure creation (Ink), a being of pure destruction was necessitated. Lezmyon represents the void that hungers to reclaim all that the Muqadas built.
The Eternal Hunt He is the most dangerous entity in the cosmos, constantly seeking to finish the job he started by hunting down the last remnants of the Muqadas bloodline.
Possession of Ink Lezmyon operates from inside Ink's body. He ate the part of Ink's brain holding long-term memory, installed Chaos Blood to prevent regeneration, and edits Ink's scarf to control what Ink remembers.

The Successor

Adham - The Last Emperor

With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Lezmyon's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
