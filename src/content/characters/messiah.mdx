## The Background

### Origin

The Messiah is a legendary figure foretold to bring true balance to the multiverse, standing above the squabbles of Angels and Emperors.

Many believe The Messiah is the only entity capable of permanently destroying Lezmyon and restoring the Golden Age.

## History & Actions

### The Connection
Adham has heard the stories, but he doesn't rely on saviors. In his experience, if you want something saved, you have to do it yourself.

### The Future
Their fate remains deeply tied to the survival of the universe and the actions of the last Emperor.

### The Power
A being possessing profound capabilities that transcend ordinary comprehension, standing as a pivotal piece in the cosmic war.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. The Messiah's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
