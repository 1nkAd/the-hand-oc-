## The Background

### Origin

Lucifer rebelled against the bureaucratic indifference of heaven. He saw the flaws in the system before anyone else did.

He now operates as a wild card, manipulating events from the shadows, opposing both Gabriel's rigid order and Lezmyon's pure chaos.

## History & Actions

### The Connection
Lucifer finds Adham deeply fascinating. The Last Emperor is a variable that neither God nor the Angels predicted.

### The Future
Their fate remains deeply tied to the survival of the universe and the actions of the last Emperor.

### The Power
A being possessing profound capabilities that transcend ordinary comprehension, standing as a pivotal piece in the cosmic war.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Lucifer's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
