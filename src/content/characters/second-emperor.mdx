# IDENTITY

Under the Second Great Emperor, the Muqadas reached the highest point of technological advancement in their history.

## The Golden Age
His reign was one of unchecked expansion. But with such rapid growth came enemies. His Golden Age was not meant to last forever, but the knowledge accumulated directly inspired the Third Emperor to build the Trees.
