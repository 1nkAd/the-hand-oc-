## The Golden Age

### Expansion and Prosperity
The Third Emperor inherited the discoveries of his predecessors and expanded them across the multiverse. Under his rule, the Muqadas reached the absolute peak of their power, establishing themselves as a dominant force of knowledge and stability.

He furthered the development of Golden Blood and laid the foundations for the weapons and archives that would define his successors, though he did not foresee the divine wrath that their ascension would eventually trigger.

## History & Actions

### The Legacy
His reign is remembered as a time of unbridled progress. The technological and spiritual advancements of the Third Emperor's era directly informed the creation of the Emperor's Summon by the Fourth Emperor, as well as the construction of the Great Tree.

### The Successor
Like all Emperors before Adham, the Third Emperor's memories were compressed and passed down through the bloodline. With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again.

### The Tree

### The Golden Archive

 A massive, golden, glowing tree of unknown location. He built it to store knowledge itself — everything every Muqadas Emperor ever learned across all of time.

 The Tree's mission was cosmic: a force intending to give every universe a genuine chance at a better life. But beneath the visible wars of the universe was a single directive from an unnamed god: destroy the Muqadas, and destroy the Tree. It was ultimately burned by Lezmyon , and all that knowledge was lost.

### History & Actions

 The Emperor's Title 
 He was the man who emerged from the Tree as the Previous Emperor of the Muqadas. He held the most hands among his race at the time, earning him the Emperor title and its associated immortality. 

 The Wipe 
 When the Wipe came and hands began consolidating, the title eventually shifted away from him. He is no longer Emperor, no longer immortal. 

 Golden Blood 
 He gave Golden Blood to Ink, ensuring the primordial entity of creation would carry the regenerative legacy of the Muqadas.
