## The Background

### Origin

Ayman, known under the moniker AGX, was Nour's closest friend during the training under the Nine. They were a duo, always operating in perfect sync.

When Nour disappeared, Ayman was left to navigate the brutal system alone. He developed a sharper, more pragmatic approach to survival.

## History & Actions

### The Connection
Adham respects Ayman's tactical genius. Though they weren't best friends, they shared a bond forged in the crucible of their impossible training.

### The Future
Their fate remains deeply tied to the survival of the universe and the actions of the last Emperor.

### The Power
A being possessing profound capabilities that transcend ordinary comprehension, standing as a pivotal piece in the cosmic war.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Ayman / AGX's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
