## The Connection

### The Meeting
Adham arrived in her universe just in time to see Jabrial sending a beam from the sky to wipe out an entire village. Linksia was just a child standing in front of it. Adham moved to protect her without deciding to.

Everything Adham does now runs through one filter: Linksia. He will do anything to protect her — no limit, no line, no hesitation. But the inverse is just as absolute: he will not do anything she would see as bad. The man who burned 1.5 billion people now measures his actions against the opinion of one child.

## Powers & Abilities

### Light Control
She can manipulate light. Full scope to be established.

### The Upgrade
Points at a person and upgrades a power or physical ability. Pushed far enough, it tears the person apart from the inside. Costs focus and stamina; sustained extreme upgrades drain her rapidly.

## History & Actions

### The Limit
Adham's absolute loyalty to Linksia acts as a strict limit on his own destructive potential. He wants to look good in her eyes. Not powerful, not feared — good.

### The Future
In the Refugee Universe, she continues to grow up safely, completely unaware of the sheer cosmic scale of the horrors Adham has committed or the powers he holds.

### The Burden
She is the only thing standing between Adham and total moral collapse. That is not weakness; it is the only anchor he has left.
