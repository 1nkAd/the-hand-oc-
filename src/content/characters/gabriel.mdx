## The Background

### Origin

Gabriel is a high-ranking Archangel, tasked with enforcing the will of a God that has seemingly abandoned the universe.

Operating entirely on a rigid, bureaucratic interpretation of divine law, Gabriel is devoid of human empathy, carrying out planetary wipes without a second thought.

## History & Actions

### The Connection
Adham clashed directly with the Archangel's forces when Jabrial (a subordinate) attempted to wipe Linksia's village. Gabriel views Adham as a glitch in the cosmic order.

### The Future
Their fate remains deeply tied to the survival of the universe and the actions of the last Emperor.

### The Power
A being possessing profound capabilities that transcend ordinary comprehension, standing as a pivotal piece in the cosmic war.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Gabriel's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
