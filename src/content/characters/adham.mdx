The last of the Muqadas. Adham is the first Emperor reborn, carrying the potential of one trillion hands. He operates as a mercenary but carries the legacy of an entire extinct race. 

## The Glitch
A small four-pointed star mark sits just below his left eye. A wish arrow designed to kill and reborn its target hit him, but because he is an immortal Emperor, the process got stuck halfway, corrupting his existence with digital distortion.

## Powers & Abilities
<Accordion title="Powers & Abilities">
- **The Hands**: Manifests glowing golden arms from his back.
- **Golden Blood**: Regenerative legacy of his people.
- **The Signature Hand**: Can replicate any attack he survives exactly once.
</Accordion>
