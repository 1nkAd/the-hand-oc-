The last of the Muqadas. Adham is the first Emperor reborn, carrying the potential of one trillion hands. He operates as a mercenary but carries the legacy of an entire extinct race. 

<Accordion title="Appearance & The Glitch">
**Hair:** Deep blue with orange highlights — not dyed, just his natural hair.

**Eyes:** Amber-gold, glowing. Scales with emotional or power state — a quiet Adham has a low steady burn. An activated Adham has eyes like lit coals.

**Glasses:** Thin, rectangular, silver-framed. Sitting low on his nose.

**The Glitch:** A small four-pointed star mark below his left eye — the entry point of Seraph's wish arrow. The arrow was designed to kill and reborn its target. It hit an immortal Emperor. The death could not complete. The rebirth could not trigger. Digital distortion crawls upward from the mark, fragmenting vision on that side with light artifacts and circuit-like interference.

**Combat:** Black tactical vest over a black turtleneck. The golden hands — glowing manifestations of his inherited Muqadas arms — emerge from behind him when his power activates, surrounding him in a ring of reaching light belonging to a trillion dead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Origin & The First Emperor">
### The Origin of Everything
Before the Muqadas were the Muqadas, there was a man. His name is not recorded anywhere that still exists. He was the first of his people — not a spacefaring civilization yet, just a planet running out of everything. 

He spent one hundred years walking, crossing dead worlds looking for something that could save what remained of his people. He found nothing. One by one, they died. By the time he reached the very last second of his own life, he was completely alone.

And then he looked up.

What he saw has no name. The universe opened. Every piece of information that had ever existed, was existing, or would ever exist flooded into him simultaneously. 

He did not die. Instead — he made a chant. The chant made him immortal. Not gifted immortality, not inherited — *made.* He wrote the rules of his own existence into the fabric of what is. He built the Tree to hold that knowledge.

### What He Saw — And What It Cost
Whatever he saw that day was not meant to be seen. A god noticed. And it decided that what he knew had to be destroyed. The Wipe was not random. Underneath all of it was a single directive: destroy the Muqadas, destroy the Tree.

### The Rebirth
Adham is the first Emperor, reborn. That is why he was born with more than one hand. He does not know any of this. The Tree burned. He is the vessel without the contents. The crown without the history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Muqadas & The Wipe">
### The Muqadas System
The Muqadas were an ancient spacefaring race built around a single religious truth: their god reached down and assigned each of them one of his one trillion hands. Every Muqadas was born with a **Number** — from 1 to 1,000,000,000,000 — and a unique power tied to that number.

When a Muqadas dies, their Number passes to a **random** living member of the race. The one with the most hands becomes **Emperor** — a title carrying a divine lock: **immortality**.

### The Wipe
A trillion people don't die from one thing. They die from everything going wrong at once.
When the Tree's mission became known — a cosmic force intending to give every universe a genuine chance at a better life — not everyone welcomed it. 

Of a trillion people, four survived. They fled to Earth and settled in Egypt. Adham was born there. They ran out of time for the truth.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Powers, Blood, and Combat">
### Fighting Style
Adham doesn't rush. He doesn't need to. He's immortal — and he uses that fact like a weapon. *Let them show you everything first.*
He takes hits. Studies them. Reads your rhythm. Then he hits back. Your own moves, returned with perfect form — or his own style, built specifically to counter everything you just showed him.

### The Signature Hand
If he takes a hit from any attack and survives, he can replicate that exact attack once — same type, same power level. He thinks of it as his only real ability. It is one of a trillion.

### The Hands
Multiple additional arms, each capable of independent movement. In combat rarely uses more than four to six. Has never pushed to see how many he can summon. The number would disturb him.

### Golden Blood
A golden rain fell from the sky, and one drop landed in the Emperor's cup. From that moment, Golden Blood ran in his veins.
Runs in Acts 1 through 100, scaling exponentially. The active side unlocks creation and regeneration abilities.
- **Act 101 — The Threshold**: Unlocked only at the edge of death, only when genuinely willing to give everything for someone else. Adham becomes a temporary conduit for every Muqadas who ever lived. He can fully heal or pull back from death's edge any number of people simultaneously.

### The Emperor's Summon
Every Emperor carried one secret that was never written down. The summon is a being that exists outside the normal hierarchy of power. Gods can be killed by it. It is the single most destructive thing the Muqadas ever had access to.

*I am the last. I am the first.*  
*The one who carries the trillion.*  
*I do not call you with power.*  
*I call you with right.*  
*The right inherited from the dead.*  
**Come out.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Relationships">
### Fie (The Wife)
She was the first person Adham met in the assassin universe who talked to him like he was just a person. Not a weapon. She remembered small things. The steadiness turned out to be a surface, and underneath it was something he hadn't seen coming. He did not fall apart. He burned. 1.5 billion people became the distance between who he was and who he became.

### Lezmyon (The Betrayer)
Before anything had a name, a single drop of blood hit paper. From it, Lezmyon woke up. He possesses Ink's body and rewrites anything he wants. He reached into Adham's family's minds with Satan's Whisper. He burned the Tree.

### Linksia (The Daughter)
The daughter of an angel and a human. Jabrial sent a beam to wipe her village. Adham threw himself between her and the beam. He survived because he always survives. She survived because he was there. He raised her. For Adham, who closed every door after Fie, Linksia is the door he forgot to close.
</Accordion>
