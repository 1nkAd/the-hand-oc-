### Creation

### The Burden of Golden Blood

 Ink is a primordial entity dedicated entirely to the act of creation. It is the direct opposite of Lezmyon. To protect it from destruction, the Tree Maker gifted Ink the Golden Blood.

 The Golden Blood ensures that Ink can regenerate and survive almost any assault. It carries the regenerative legacy of the Muqadas within its veins, acting as a living memorial to the extinct race.

### History & Actions

 The Nature of Creation 
 Ink creates instinctively. Its entire existence revolves around breathing life into new realities and crafting new universes. 

 The Endless War 
 Ink is locked in an eternal, cosmic struggle with Lezmyon. Whatever Ink creates, Lezmyon seeks to burn. 

 Golden Blood 
 The gift from the Tree Maker was not just for survival, but a symbol of hope. The Muqadas believed that as long as Ink lived, their vision of a better multiverse would never truly die. 

 The Scarf & Memory 
 Lezmyon ate the part of Ink's brain that holds long-term memory. Ink wakes up with no past and writes everything he learns on a limitless scarf. When Lezmyon takes control, he rewrites the scarf. 

 Chaos Blood Lock 
 Lezmyon installed Chaos Blood inside Ink's body as a lock. It constantly destroys any attempt by Ink's Golden Blood to regenerate his lost memory.
