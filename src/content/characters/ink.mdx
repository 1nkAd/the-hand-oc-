# IDENTITY

Ink is the primordial entity of creation, locked in an eternal war with his mirror, Lezmyon. 

## The Living Page
Anything Ink draws or writes becomes fully real and independent. 

## The Burden
To protect him, the Tree Maker gifted Ink the Golden Blood. However, Lezmyon installed Chaos Blood inside Ink's body as a lock, preventing his long-term memory from ever regenerating.
