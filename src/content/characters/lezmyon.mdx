 Lezmyon is a force born from a single drop of blood. He is the antithesis of Ink. Where Ink creates, Lezmyon destroys.

 He was responsible for the ultimate destruction of the Golden Archive. By burning the Tree, he erased billions of years of accumulated Muqadas knowledge, forcing Adham to become the last remaining backup of their legacy.

### History & Actions

 The Nature of Ruin 
 He is not a person in the traditional sense, but an inevitable consequence of the universe's mechanics. He embodies betrayal and the erasure of history. 

 The Cosmic Balance 
 In a universe with a being of pure creation (Ink), a being of pure destruction was necessitated. Lezmyon represents the void that hungers to reclaim all that the Muqadas built. 

 The Eternal Hunt 
 He is the most dangerous entity in the cosmos, constantly seeking to finish the job he started by hunting down the last remnants of the Muqadas bloodline. 

 Possession of Ink 
 Lezmyon operates from inside Ink's body. He ate the part of Ink's brain holding long-term memory, installed Chaos Blood to prevent regeneration, and edits Ink's scarf to control what Ink remembers.
