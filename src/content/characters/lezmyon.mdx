# IDENTITY

Lezmyon is a primordial entity born from a single drop of blood. Where Ink creates, Lezmyon destroys.

## The Betrayal
He was responsible for the ultimate destruction of the Golden Archive. By burning the Tree, he erased billions of years of accumulated Muqadas knowledge.

## Powers & Abilities
- **Chaos Blood**: The inverse of Golden Blood. It heals by destroying and constantly burns the body.
- **Satan's Whisper**: Full psychological possession. Reaches into a person's mind and rewrites what they feel and believe.
