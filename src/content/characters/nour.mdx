## The Rebellion

### Hatred of the System

Ink dragged him out of his story at the end of it. Nour was furious. He hated everything about the system — not because he wanted to do evil, but because he wanted to do good on his own terms.

Light is the mirror of what the Nine claim to stand for. They say they exist to lead the world toward good by example and choice. Light is proof that even the Nine's system can strip someone of choice while claiming to serve it. He wanted to be good. He just wanted it to be his.

## History & Actions

### The Escape
After the Tree burned, Light finally got out. He found a universe, found a girl — an idol named Ei — and started a family.

### The Disappearance
They had twins: Yuro (male) and Yuno (female), who carry his features mixed with hers. Then he disappeared. No explanation. No goodbye.

### The Void Left Behind
His sudden absence left Ayman and the others reeling. Nour was always the moral compass of the group, and without him, things changed.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Nour (Light)'s legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
