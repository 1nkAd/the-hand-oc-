## The Background

### Origin

Ahmed was brought in by Lezmyon to train under the Nine. He possessed a terrifying potential but preferred the quiet.

Unlike Nour or Ayman, Ahmed never tried to be the center of attention. He operated in the shadows, mastering his powers in solitude.

## History & Actions

### The Connection
He was part of Adham's group. The group worked because nobody forced it, and Ahmed's silent presence was the glue that kept them grounded.

### The Future
Their fate remains deeply tied to the survival of the universe and the actions of the last Emperor.

### The Power
A being possessing profound capabilities that transcend ordinary comprehension, standing as a pivotal piece in the cosmic war.

## The Successor

### Adham - The Last Emperor
With the Tree burned, Adham is now the only place that knowledge can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. Ahmed's legacy rests entirely inside an assassin who doesn't know he's carrying it.
