## APPEARANCE

**Hair:** Deep blue with orange highlights — not dyed, just his natural hair.

**Eyes:** Amber-gold, glowing. Scales with emotional or power state — a quiet Adham has a low steady burn. An activated Adham has eyes like lit coals.

**Glasses:** Thin, rectangular, silver-framed. Sitting low on his nose.

**Mark & The Glitch:** A small four-pointed star mark sits just below his left eye, the entry point of Seraph's wish arrow. The arrow was designed to kill and reborn its target. It hit an immortal Emperor. The death could not complete. The rebirth could not trigger. The process got stuck halfway and corrupted something deeper than flesh; not a wound, but a glitch in Adham's existence itself. Golden Blood cannot heal it because it heals damage. This is not damage. It is a broken state. Digital distortion crawls upward from the mark across his left eye, fragmenting vision on that side with light artifacts and circuit-like interference. It will be resolved later in the story.

**Build:** Lean and composed. Not someone who looks dangerous until he is.

**Combat:** Black tactical vest over a black turtleneck. The golden hands — glowing manifestations of his inherited Muqadas arms — emerge from behind him when his power activates, surrounding him in a ring of reaching light belonging to a trillion dead.

**Casual:** Black turtleneck or sweater. Same glasses. Looks like someone you'd pass on the street and forget until you remembered the eyes.

**Default expression:** Hand raised to cover his mouth. Not nervous — evaluating. The eyes do all the talking before he decides to.

## THE FIRST EMPEROR — THE ORIGIN OF EVERYTHING

*(classified — unknown to all characters)*

Before the Muqadas were the Muqadas, there was a man. His name is not recorded anywhere that still exists. He was the first of his people — not a spacefaring civilization yet, just a planet running out of everything. He was extraordinarily intelligent, possessed a will that no one around him could fully explain, and under his leadership his people reached heights no other civilization in their region of existence had touched.

Then the resources ran out.

He spent one hundred years walking — crossing dead worlds, empty systems, the cold between stars — looking for something that could save what remained of his people. He found nothing. One by one, they died. All of them. By the time he reached the very last second of his own life, his brain already failing, his body already going, he was completely alone.

And then he looked up.

What he saw has no name. It should not have been visible to any living thing. In that final fraction of a second — with his mind breaking down, all filters gone, all limits dissolved — the universe opened. Every piece of information that had ever existed, was existing, or would ever exist flooded into him simultaneously. The complete record of all things, in an instant.

He did not die. Instead — he made a chant. The words came from somewhere deeper than thought, shaped by everything he had just seen. The chant made him immortal. Not gifted immortality, not inherited — *made.* He wrote the rules of his own existence into the fabric of what is.

He came back. He rebuilt. He created the Muqadas — the hand system, the Number inheritance, all of it — from the knowledge of what he had seen. He built the Tree to hold that knowledge, passing it from Emperor to Emperor so it would never be lost again.

**What He Saw — And What It Cost**
Whatever he saw that day was not meant to be seen. A god — unnamed, nature and origin unknown to any surviving record — noticed. And it decided that what he knew had to be destroyed. Not him, not his people. The *information.* The knowledge stored in the Tree.

The Wipe was not random. The faction wars, the bio-weapons, the cascading disasters — they were the visible layer. Underneath all of it was a single directive, passed through intermediaries across generations by this god: destroy the Muqadas, destroy the Tree, destroy what the first Emperor saw. The last move in that campaign was a messenger — sent by this god — who found Lezmyon and told him to burn the Tree. That is why Lezmyon did it. He was the final instrument of something far older and far more deliberate than him.

**The Rebirth — And The Empty Vessel**
Adham is the first Emperor, reborn. That is why he was born with more than one hand — the race's own system, built by the first Emperor himself, identified him when he arrived. The Numbers moved toward him not by chance but by something written into the design from the beginning.

He does not know any of this.

The first Emperor stored everything he knew in the Tree — every memory, every truth, every fragment of what he saw at the edge of death. It was meant to be passed down, Emperor to Emperor, until the rebirth arrived and could reclaim it. The Tree burned. The memories burned with it. Adham carries the soul of the first Emperor and none of his knowledge. He is the vessel without the contents. The crown without the history.

The god that sent the messenger does not know Adham exists. It believes the information is gone. It believes it won.

The prophecy:

*"Rest, my child, as you are the beginning and the end.*
*As you will regain what's lost, you will remember who you are.*
*The Emperor must rule.*
*The first one is back and will be forever."*

## THE FIVE GREAT EMPERORS OF THE MUQADAS

The Muqadas Empire spanned a trillion lives and an age beyond counting. Of all the Emperors who ever ruled, five stand above the rest — not by power alone, but by what they left behind. Four of them changed what it meant to be Muqadas. One of them created the Muqadas entirely.

---

**THE FIRST — THE ONE WHO SAW**
*(Above classification. The origin of everything.)*

He is not counted among the four great Emperors — he is above them. He is the beginning. Before the Muqadas existed, before the hand system, before Golden Blood, before the Tree — there was one man on a dying planet who walked for a hundred years and looked up at the wrong moment. The universe opened. He survived it. He built everything that came after. Every Emperor who ever lived, every Number ever assigned, every hand ever inherited — all of it traces back to him.

He is the reason Adham exists. He is what Adham is.

---

**THE FIRST GREAT EMPEROR — THE GOLDEN RAIN**

In the age of the first great Emperor, no rain fell for four hundred years. The civilization prayed without answer for four centuries. Then a golden rain came from the sky — one drop landed in the Emperor's cup. He drank it. Golden Blood entered the Muqadas bloodline from that moment forward, passed through every generation until the Wipe. He did not create it — he received it, and he gave it to his people. Every act of healing, every regeneration, every pull toward good that any Muqadas ever felt traces back to this Emperor and the drop in his cup.

---

**THE SECOND GREAT EMPEROR — THE GOLDEN AGE**

Under the second great Emperor, the Muqadas reached the highest point of technological advancement in their history — surpassing anything any other civilization in their known universe had achieved. The details of what they built, what they discovered, and how far they reached are not fully recorded anywhere that still exists. What is known is that this was the peak. Everything after was maintenance of what he built, and everything before was preparation for it.

---

**THE THIRD GREAT EMPEROR — THE BUILDER OF TREES**

The third great Emperor built the Trees — the golden, glowing archives that stored the accumulated knowledge of every Emperor who came before. He took the first Emperor's vision, the Golden Age's discoveries, and everything the Muqadas had ever learned, and gave it a permanent home. The Tree that the Tree Maker (the previous Emperor) tended was built on the foundation this Emperor created. When Lezmyon burned the Tree, he burned the culmination of this Emperor's life's work.

---

**THE FOURTH GREAT EMPEROR — THE SUMMON**

The fourth great Emperor created the Emperor's Summon — the weapon capable of killing gods, passed in secret from Emperor to Emperor, never written down. He created it as the ultimate safeguard for the Muqadas people. No threat, no god, no force in existence could wipe them out as long as the Emperor carried this. He was right about the power. He was wrong about the control. The last Emperor who used it destroyed his own people with it. The fourth great Emperor could not have known that would happen — he built a shield. What it became was something else.

---

The Muqadas were an ancient spacefaring race built around a single religious truth: their god reached down and assigned each of them one of his one trillion hands. Every Muqadas was born with a **Number** — from 1 to 1,000,000,000,000 — and a unique power tied to that number.

**The Inheritance System**
When a Muqadas dies, their Number passes to a **random** living member of the race, along with an additional hand. No choosing, no bloodline, no destiny. The hands fall where they fall. The one with the most hands becomes **Emperor** — a title carrying a divine lock: **immortality**. The Emperor cannot die. When a new Muqadas is born, the universe balances itself — the newborn takes one hand at random from any living member who carries more than one. Even the Emperor is not exempt.

Except Adham. There is no one left to be born. There is no one left to die and scatter their hands elsewhere. Every Number has drifted to him, and the tide has nowhere left to go.

**Adham's Birth — Destined Emperor**
When Adham was born, something unprecedented happened. For the first time in Muqadas history, a newborn took more than one hand at birth — pulling the consolidated 999,999,999,996 extinct Numbers that had naturally funneled into his surviving family members during the Wipe. His parents knew immediately: he was destined to be Emperor. Not by chance — written into the race's design from the beginning. They said nothing. He was a child, in hiding. They would tell him at twenty, as tradition demanded. They ran out of time.

**What Adham Doesn't Know**
He is the last Muqadas alive. He was always going to be Emperor. His birth announced it. The Emperor is immortal. He has been dying and surviving for years and assumes he is simply lucky.

## THE WIPE

A trillion people don't die from one thing. They die from everything going wrong at once.

When the Tree's mission became known — a cosmic force intending to give every universe a genuine chance at a better life — not everyone welcomed it. Beneath the visible wars and bio-weapons was a single directive from an unnamed god: destroy the Muqadas, destroy the Tree, destroy what the first Emperor saw. The faction wars were real. The chaos-aligned races were real. But they were instruments of something older and more deliberate.

The decisive blow was biological: weapons engineered specifically around Muqadas physiology, spreading in waves through a civilization already destabilized by conflict. A cure was developed and never distributed. A ceasefire collapsed days before a critical resupply. A migration of survivors reached a contaminated system.

Not conspiracy on the surface. Just the universe failing to leave enough space between disasters for anyone to breathe. But underneath — a god was running the board.

Of a trillion people, four survived. A family — mother, father, brother, and sister. They fled to Earth and settled in Egypt. Adham was born there. They tried to give him something ordinary before they could give him the truth. They ran out of time for the truth.

## POWERS & ABILITIES

Adham's full power set is theoretically limitless — one trillion unique abilities inherited from a dead race. He operates on instinct. He is an ocean who thinks he's a glass of water.

---

**The Clock Hands — Lona's Inheritance**
Four clock hands total — two given to him by Lona during Balance's test, two taken from her after she died.

- **Physical Manifestation:** Solid, separate physical constructs divided into two pairs.
- **Absolute Weight:** So incomprehensibly heavy that only Adham is capable of lifting or moving them. To any other entity, they are immovable objects.
- **Temporal Function:** Positioned like clock hands, they manipulate time directly — Stop (freeze time entirely), Forward (accelerate time). Further applications to be established.
- **The Cost:** Moving time requires strength beyond what most beings can generate. Adham can move them because he has subjectively experienced billions of years of physical conditioning, accumulating strength beyond any natural ceiling — channeled together with all one trillion inherited Muqadas hands compressed into a single point. With this combined force he can stop time for approximately two to three minutes before the strain forces release.

---

**The Rewrite Key**
Received from Balance after killing Lona. Rewrites one thing in history. Two absolute rules: (1) cannot affect any of the four gods of this universe; (2) cannot affect any previous or future use of another Rewrite Key. Before she died, Lona told him not to use it selfishly. He hasn't used it yet. Full mechanics in the Balance section.

---

**The Signature Hand**
If he takes a hit from any attack and survives, he can replicate that exact attack once — same type, same power level. He thinks of it as his only real ability. It is one of a trillion.
- **Storage Limit:** He can only hold exactly one replicated attack at a time. Absorbing a new attack while one is stored overwrites the previous one.

**Golden Blood**

*Origin:* The Muqadas racial power system did not always exist. There was a period in Muqadas history — long before the Wipe — where no rain fell for four hundred years. The entire civilization prayed to their god for four centuries without answer. Then a golden rain fell from the sky. One drop landed in the Emperor's cup. He drank it. From that moment, Golden Blood ran in his veins — and anyone who drank his blood received the system too, starting from Act 1. It passed through generations, becoming the foundation of Muqadas biology. By the time of the Wipe, every living Muqadas carried it. Adham carries it now as the last of them — and still believes Lezmyon gave it to him.

*Mechanics:* Runs in Acts 1 through 100, scaling exponentially. Each new tier is categorically different from the last. The passive side amplifies all stats. The active side unlocks creation and regeneration abilities growing in scope with each tier. Golden Blood cannot be weaponized to harm directly. The deeper someone goes into its Acts, the stronger their biological pull toward doing good becomes. Not compulsion — pressure. Golden Blood pushes you toward good and makes you want to do good, but it cannot force you. Overwhelming emotional trauma combined with a genuine belief that what you are doing is right — even if that belief is distorted — is enough to override it.

| 6–10 | Enhanced | Create small helpful organisms |
| 11–15 | Enhanced | Previous tier at greater scale |
| 16–20 | Enhanced | Create any non-living, non-complex matter |
| 21–25 | Enhanced | Previous tier at greater scale |
| 26–30 | Enhanced | Regenerate damaged or missing organs |
| 31–35 | Enhanced | Previous tier at greater scale |

| 96–100 | Peak amplification | Full body regeneration at extreme speed; create anything causing no harm |
| **101** | **Unique** | **Unique** |

*Act 101 — The Threshold:* Unlocked only at the edge of death, only when the person is genuinely willing to give everything for someone else — not survival, not revenge. Emotional condition, not physical. Every Act 101 is unique to the person.

*Prior Use:* Adham has used Act 101 at least once before during his multi-universe travels. The memory of that use — who he saved, why, what it cost — was lost when the rebirth arrow glitched his existence. He does not remember he has ever used it. The 100 year cooldown may or may not have expired by the current arc.

*Adham's Act 101 — "The Last Emperor":* He becomes a temporary conduit for every Muqadas who ever lived. The trillion dead speak through him — as pure restoration. He can fully heal or pull back from death's edge any number of people simultaneously. He cannot use it on himself. He cannot weaponize it. The cost: burns as close to empty as his body allows. Wakes up days later with nothing left. Cooldown: one use every 100 years. The man who killed 1.5 billion people has a transcendence ability that exclusively saves lives — he bypassed the Golden Blood pull through overwhelming grief and a genuine belief, however distorted, that he was sparing those people from suffering.

*Sharing Golden Blood:* Anyone who drinks Adham's blood receives Golden Blood, starting from Act 1. Whether they ever reach 101 depends entirely on who they are.

**Chaos Blood — The Inverse System**
Same 101 Acts, same scaling, same healing speed — everything else a mirror flipped into darkness. Heals by destroying: every cell burns and dies like cancer, replaced by new cancerous cells. Always regenerating, always in pain. Where Golden Blood creates, Chaos Blood destroys at identical scale and precision. The deeper someone progresses, the stronger their pull toward destruction becomes. By Act 101 the pull is extreme but not absolute — just as Golden Blood can be overridden, so can Chaos Blood. It pushes and makes you want to destroy, but it cannot force you. Reverse-engineered by Lezmyon from studying Golden Blood.

---

**The Hands** — Multiple additional arms, each capable of independent movement. In combat rarely uses more than four to six. Has never pushed to see how many he can summon. The number would disturb him.

**Physical Amplification** — Strength, speed, durability far beyond human limits.

**Residual Power Surfacing** — In extreme stress or emotion, a Number bleeds through that he's never consciously accessed. Unpredictable and sometimes catastrophic.

**Immortality** — Does not age. Wounds heal. Has been killed more than once. Always wakes up. He calls it luck.

**The 1T Ceiling** — The day he learns the truth of his race, his Number system, and his title — that is the day the ceiling disappears. He is currently the only place the accumulated knowledge of every Muqadas Emperor can exist. The Tree is burned. He is the last backup. And somewhere beneath the billion years of travel and grief, the first Emperor's memories are still in there — waiting.

**THE EMPEROR'S SUMMON** *(classified — unknown to all characters, including Adham)*

Every Emperor of the Muqadas carried one secret that was never written down, never spoken aloud, never passed through the inheritance system. It was given directly — Emperor to Emperor — and died with the line when the Wipe came. Adham has never been told. He does not know it exists.

The summon is a being — or a force wearing the shape of one — that exists outside the normal hierarchy of power. Gods can be killed by it. It is the single most destructive thing the Muqadas ever had access to, kept because having it meant no one could ever truly threaten the race at an existential level. They were wrong about that.

*What Actually Happened During the Wipe:* When it became clear the race was going to lose, the Emperor summoned it. It cannot be controlled — this was always known. He summoned it anyway. It destroyed their enemies. It also destroyed the Muqadas. The Emperor survived because the Emperor is immortal. His entire race did not. The four survivors escaped only because they were already gone before it reached them. They never knew what finished the Wipe.

Adham carries the ability to summon it somewhere in his one trillion inherited powers. He has no idea it exists.

## FIE — THE WIFE

Her name was Fie.

She was the first person Adham met in the assassin universe who talked to him like he was just a person. Not a weapon, not a rumor. Just a person who looked tired and was bad at small talk. No powers. Didn't know what he was. She knew he worked in security and asked once what that was like, then never asked again because she could read the answer in how long he took to respond.

What she had was steadiness. She remembered small things. She had opinions about music and shared them without being asked. She laughed at the wrong moments in films and didn't apologize for it. She made the apartment feel like somewhere worth coming back to.

**The Memory He Keeps**
A Tuesday. She is sitting on the kitchen counter — "chairs are for guests and I live here" — talking about something he has already forgotten the subject of. He is not really listening. He is just watching her talk, thinking: *this is the world. This right here. Whatever else exists, this is the part that counts.* He has never told anyone about that moment.

**The End**
She left. What matters is that the steadiness turned out to be a surface, and underneath it was something he hadn't seen coming because he had spent so long watching for threats from the outside that he forgot to account for the ones that arrive quietly and know where you sleep. He did not fall apart. He burned. 1.5 billion people became the distance between who he was on that Tuesday in the kitchen and who he was capable of being when the last soft thing he owned was taken. He does not blame her anymore. He doesn't have the energy. What remains is not anger — just the absence of the thing that used to be there, which is its own kind of permanent.

## LEZMYON — THE BETRAYER

**Origin**
Before the universe. Before anything had a name — a single drop of ink fell on paper. From it, **Ink** rose. In the same moment, a single drop of blood hit the same paper. From it, **Lezmyon** woke up. They are not brothers. Not enemies, exactly. They are the two things that existed before existence — creation and ruin, born from the same instant, the same surface, the same silence.

---

### INK

Ink is not human. He is a primordial entity of creation — the living remnant of that first drop that gave form to everything.

For an unknowable stretch of time, Ink and Lezmyon existed together. Then a man emerged from a tree — the Well — speaking about purpose, about giving every universe and every living thing a genuine chance. Lezmyon knew immediately he could not beat him. Not yet. So he listened, smiled, and waited.

The man gave Ink Golden Blood. When he left, Lezmyon studied it, dismantled it, and built its mirror. That is how Chaos Blood was born — not discovered, not inherited, but reverse-engineered from something meant for creation.

Then Lezmyon ate the part of Ink's brain that holds long-term memory. Then he installed Chaos Blood inside Ink's body as a lock — not a weapon. Ink carries Golden Blood, a regenerative system that, left alone, would eventually rebuild what was taken. The Chaos Blood burns every attempt down. Ink heals everything except the one thing that matters.

**The Scarf**
Ink wakes up with no past. All he has is a scarf — fits any neck perfectly, when unfolded has no end. He writes everything he learns on it before Lezmyon surfaces again. When Lezmyon takes control, he rewrites anything he doesn't want Ink to know. He edits the only memory Ink has left.

**Ink's Power — The Living Page**
Anything Ink draws or writes becomes real. Fully real, fully independent. This is how Dream and Fresh exist. He did not create the Muqadas' original universe or the god-left timeline — those exist independently.

**The Training**
The Well contacted Lezmyon — operating as Ink — to bring four kids in to train. Lezmyon had no choice. Refusing would risk the Tree discovering he was inside Ink's body. He completed the mission on his own terms — Adham arrived broken, family gone, past rewritten — but he arrived. The four kids know only Ink. They trust him, bond with him, have no idea their mentor is a prisoner watching through someone else's eyes.

**Ink & Lezmyon**
Ink is fully conscious when Lezmyon operates. He watches everything. He just can't always hold onto what he's seen. And yet he loves his brother — the way something ancient and unconditional doesn't have an off switch. Lezmyon loves him back. In his own broken logic, he does. If there were a version of his goals that let him keep Ink whole, he would take it. He simply doesn't believe that version exists. Two primordial beings from the same moment, the same paper, the same silence — and the closest thing either of them has ever had to home is the other one.

Like all Emperors, his immortality was tied to that title — and the title is not permanent. It passes to whoever accumulates the most hands by random inheritance. When the Wipe came and hands began consolidating, the title eventually shifted. He is no longer Emperor, no longer immortal, and his current status is unknown. He built the Tree, gave Golden Blood to Ink, and walked back into the branches.

That knowledge is gone. Adham is now the only place it can ever exist again — if he ever unlocks what he is. And buried somewhere beneath his surface, the first Emperor's memories of what he saw at the edge of death are still waiting.

## BACKSTORY — UNIVERSE BY UNIVERSE

**Origin Universe — Egypt, Earth**
Normal school. Normal life. Powers surfaced quietly. When Adham was sixteen, Lezmyon — wearing Ink's body — came, used Satan's Whisper on his family, handed him a rusty knife, and forced a conclusion. Adham has never once suspected they were manipulated. As far as he knows, his family simply hated him, and when things reached a breaking point, he survived and they didn't. That is the version of events he has carried ever since. It is the wound underneath every other wound.

**The Training**
Lezmyon brought Adham in alongside three other kids. Each assigned to one of the Nine as their primary teacher. Adham trained under the Well directly. Late nights, inside jokes, people becoming important to each other before they understand what's coming.

**The Training Universes**
Universe after universe, stripped of his powers beyond immortality in most of them. He trained. He fought. He lost. He woke up and trained again. He became something very quiet and very dangerous. During this time he subjectively experienced billions of years, accumulating physical conditioning beyond any natural ceiling.

**Linksia's Universe** *(universe name and timeline placement to be established)*
At some point during his travels, Adham was investigating reports of something unusual in an unnamed universe. He arrived to find Jabrial — the same pan-dimensional entity he would later kill an instance of in the assassin universe — sending a beam from the sky to wipe an entire village. He saw Linksia in front of it. He moved without deciding to. Full details in the Linksia section.

**Balance's Test — Lona**
At some point during his travels, Balance selected Adham and sent him Lona — the female version of him, created for the test. She knew the whole time she was going to die by his hand. She did not tell him. She fought him, gave him two clock hands, and before the end told him one thing: don't use the key selfishly. When Balance forced the conclusion, Adham killed her and took her remaining clock hands. He received the Rewrite Key. Whether he fully understands what she knew and chose not to say — that is unresolved.

**The Assassin Universe — The World God Left**
God had simply left. Angels remained, cold and bureaucratic. Adham became the best assassin this world had ever produced. Never caught. Never identified. He did it for Fie — the proof he was still a person. Then she cheated on him. 1.5 billion people died. He built a mountain with his hands in days and sent a message to the only authority left worth addressing. The leader of the angels — **Seraph** — answered. Adham killed Jabrial, the mightiest of the angels. Then Seraph fired a wish arrow — built to kill and reborn its target. Against an immortal Emperor it could not complete. The death half-failed. The rebirth never started. The glitch remained. He woke up in a new universe.

## WORLD MECHANICS & CLARIFICATIONS

**Jabrial — Nature & Resurrection**
Jabrial is not a singular being that can be permanently destroyed. He is an angel made of light. **Fresh & The Clock Hands — Domain Limits**
Fresh is God of Time, but only within the domain of the Nine's universe. In the Refugee Universe, time is governed by Balance through a massive physical clock. The clock hands operate on that same physical principle — their resistance is mechanical, not mystical. No domain of godhood bypasses raw physical mass. Adham can move them because he has subjectively experienced billions of years of physical conditioning, accumulating strength beyond any natural ceiling, channeled together with all one trillion inherited Muqadas hands.

## BALANCE & THE REWRITE KEY

Balance is **Seraphim** — one of the Father's original six angels, the one who created time, matter, and balance itself. He is not a serious god. He enjoys himself. He meddles, designs tests, creates impossible situations just to see what happens.

**The Test**
Balance selects a chosen individual and sends them an **Assistant** — a version of that person in the opposite gender. The Assistant is real: their own consciousness, their own powers, their own presence. They know they are going to die — and that it will be at the hands of the chosen one. What they choose to do with that knowledge is entirely their own. At the end of the test, the chosen one must kill the Assistant. No negotiation. No alternative. After the Assistant dies, the chosen one receives the Rewrite Key.

**The Rewrite Key**
Rewrites one thing in history. Two absolute rules:
1. Cannot affect any of the four gods of this universe.
2. Cannot affect any previous or future use of another Rewrite Key.

*Adham's Key:* Received after killing Lona, his Assistant. She fought him, gave him two clock hands during the test, and told him one thing before the end: don't use it selfishly. She knew the whole time she was going to die by his hand. She did not tell him. He hasn't used the key yet.

## لينكسيا — LINKSIA

**What She Is**
The daughter of an angel and a human. In the universe where she was born, this was a sin. Jabrial — the exact same pan-dimensional entity Adham later killed an instance of in the assassin universe — sent a beam from the sky to wipe her entire village. Not just her — every witness, to make sure nothing survived that could carry the record. Adham was already in that universe investigating reports of something unusual when the beam came down. He saw it. He saw her — a child standing in front of it. He moved without deciding to. He threw himself between her and the beam and took it. He survived because he always survives. She survived because he was there.

This happened after Fie. After everything. The man who had just proven he could burn 1.5 billion people threw himself in front of divine fire for a child he had never met, without hesitation, because she was standing there and the beam was coming. He has never fully examined what that says about him.

**Their Relationship**
He raised her. Not because anyone asked, not as penance — just because after the beam there was a child with nowhere to go and he was the one standing there. She calls him father. He does not correct her. She is the only person in his current life who talks to him like he is just a person. She has no idea what he has done. She knows he is strange and strong and quiet and that he always comes back. That is enough for her.

For Adham, who closed every door after Fie, Linksia is the door he forgot to close. She is the reason Act 101 is theoretically possible again. She is the only person in his current life he would burn as close to empty as his body allows for. He would not say this. But it is true.

**Appearance**
Looks fully human. The only things that aren't are the small pointed features on top of her head — subtle enough to miss in a crowd. Light, almost white wavy hair. Dark sharp eyes with a resting expression that reads as calm but lands closer to intensity. Athletic build, visibly strong. Default: dark crop top, loose wrap pants, arms behind her head, completely unbothered.

**What She Knows**
She knows Adham is her father — the man who stepped in front of divine fire and stayed. She knows who she is: half angel, the reason a village was erased, the sin that was supposed to be corrected. She does not know what Adham actually is. She knows he is hard to hurt, quiet in a way that has something heavy behind it, and that he always comes back.

**Powers**

*Light Control* — She can manipulate light. Full scope to be established.

*The Upgrade* — She points at a person and begins upgrading something they have — a power, a sense, a physical ability. Starts small and helpful: sharper vision, faster legs. She can keep going. The upgrade does not stop unless she stops it. Push far enough and the thing being enhanced starts tearing the person apart from the inside — overwhelmed, overloaded, damaged or killed by the best version of something they used to have. The ability costs her focus and stamina proportional to how far she pushes it. Small upgrades are effortless. Sustained or extreme upgrades drain her rapidly. She cannot push indefinitely without consequence on her own end.

## PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Adham is not broken. Broken things don't function. He functions extremely well — professional, precise, economically motivated. He takes jobs, completes them, gets paid.

Underneath that: the settled kind of pain, the kind that becomes part of your structure. He likes money because money is honest. It doesn't pretend to care about him. The transaction is the whole relationship and he finds that clean.

He is not cruel — he doesn't enjoy suffering. But he has a very high threshold for what he considers necessary, and that threshold was recalibrated by grief a long time ago.

## THE HIDDEN TRUTH (DRAMATIC IRONY)

He thinks his powers came from the man who destroyed him. They are his by blood and inheritance — and they are the legacy of the first Emperor he is the rebirth of.

He thinks surviving is luck. It is divine law written into his title.

He thinks his family hated him. They were the last people who knew what he truly was, and they died holding that secret to protect him.

He thinks he is alone. He is a monarch — the last of a lineage a trillion souls wide, the rebirth of the man who built that lineage from nothing, now compressed into one person taking contracts because he likes the money.

He credits his entire racial power system to Lezmyon. It was given to his race by a rain from the sky four hundred years before he was born.

The Muqadas are gone. But their Emperor is not. And the Emperor — by every law they ever lived under — cannot die until he chooses to understand what he is.

He has not chosen yet.
